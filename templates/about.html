About ReviewRadar

Advanced sentiment analysis platform for customer feedback

What is ReviewRadar?

ReviewRadar is a comprehensive sentiment analysis platform designed to help businesses and researchers understand customer feedback through advanced machine learning techniques. Our platform processes customer reviews and provides detailed insights into sentiment patterns.

Whether you're analyzing product reviews, customer service feedback, or social media comments, ReviewRadar provides the tools you need to extract meaningful insights from text data.

How It Works

  1. Upload: Upload your CSV file containing customer reviews
  2. Process: Our system preprocesses and analyzes each review
  3. Analyze: Advanced ML models determine sentiment polarity
  4. Visualize: Interactive charts and word clouds display results
  5. Export: Download comprehensive reports in PDF or CSV format

Sentiment Analysis Process

Text Preprocessing

  • Lowercase Conversion: Standardize text case
  • Punctuation Removal: Clean special characters
  • Stopword Elimination: Remove common words
  • Lemmatization: Reduce words to base form
  • Tokenization: Split text into individual words

Sentiment Classification

  • Polarity Analysis: Determine positive/negative/neutral sentiment
  • Multi-class Classification: Positive, Negative, Neutral
  • Context Awareness: Consider surrounding words
